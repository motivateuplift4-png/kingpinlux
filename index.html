About KINGPIN LUX

Iced-out moissanite watches

Watches iced from bezel to clasp

KINGPIN LUX makes iced-out watches for people who want to be seen. Every piece is covered in VVS moissanite, set stone by stone across the bezel, the case and the full bracelet, so the watch throws light from every angle and from across the room.

Why moissanite?

Moissanite is a lab-grown gemstone made of silicon carbide. It is one of the hardest gems there is, just below diamond, which makes it ideal for a watch you wear every day. It also has more fire than diamond: the rainbow flashes you see when it catches the light. Our stones are graded VVS, so no flaws are visible to the naked eye.

Six collections, one standard

Choose your silhouette: the open-worked Phantom skeleton, the soft-cornered Monarch cushion, the eight-sided Regent, the Apex chronograph with three sub-dials, the architectural Empire square, or the round Crown classic. Most designs come in iced white, yellow, rose and two-tone finishes.

Delivered insured, worldwide

Every order ships free, tracked and fully insured, within 24 hours of your bank transfer arriving. Each watch comes in the signature KINGPIN LUX box with its certificate of authenticity, a lifetime warranty on stone setting and movement, and 30 days to return it if it isn't right.
