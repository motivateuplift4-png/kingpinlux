Tracking

Your tracking link is emailed the moment your watch leaves us, so you can follow it to your door.

Packaging

Each watch arrives in the signature KINGPIN LUX box with its certificate of authenticity.

Returns

If it isn't right, you can return it within 30 days of delivery.

  • The watch must be in its original condition, with the box and certificate.
  • Contact us first so we can give you the return details.
  • Your refund goes back to the bank account you paid from, usually within 5 business days of the watch reaching us.

Lifetime warranty

Your warranty covers stone setting and movement for as long as you own the piece. If a stone loosens or the movement needs attention, we repair or replace it.

It doesn't cover accidental damage, or normal wear to the finish over time.

Paying by bank transfer

We accept bank transfer only. Nothing is charged automatically, and no card details are ever entered on this site.

How it works

  • Place your order with your contact and delivery details. Nothing is charged.
  • Get your details: you immediately see our bank details, the exact amount and a unique order reference (KPL-…). You can save or print them.
  • Send the transfer from your own bank and put the reference in the transfer description, so we can match it to your order.
  • We ship: once the money arrives we confirm by email and dispatch within 24 hours.

Good to know

  • Your order is held for 72 hours while the transfer arrives.
  • Prices are in US dollars. Send the exact amount and choose OUR for bank charges so the full total reaches us.
  • International transfers usually take 1–3 business days to arrive.

Contact us

Questions about a piece, your order or delivery to your country? Speak to us directly. We reply personally.

Privacy

We only collect what we need to deliver your order, and we never sell your data.

What we collect

When you place an order: your name, email, phone number, delivery address, the pieces you ordered, and whether you asked to hear about new pieces.

Why

To reserve and deliver your order, match your bank transfer to it, and contact you about it. We only send you news and offers if you ticked the box at checkout.

Where it's kept

Our order system runs on Vercel, and order records are stored with Upstash. Your shopping bag and recently viewed watches are saved in your own browser only. This site doesn't use advertising or analytics cookies.

Email list

If you join the KINGPIN list, we keep your email address to send you news about new pieces and offers. Ask us to remove it at any time.

Payments

You pay by transfer from your own bank, so we never see or store card details.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete the details we hold about you at any time, using the contact details above.
